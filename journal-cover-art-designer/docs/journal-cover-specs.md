## Focal Composition Rule
Place the primary scientific structure inside the **Focal Live Window** (`x ∈ [18%, 82%]`, `y ∈ [14%, 72%]` of full-bleed height) while allowing background field lines/atmospheric geometry to extend continuously across the entire full-bleed canvas (`0% -> 100%`) so the masthead sits cleanly over dark/uncluttered upper space.
