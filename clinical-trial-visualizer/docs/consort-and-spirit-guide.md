# CONSORT Flow & SPIRIT Clinical Trial Visual Standards

## 1. CONSORT 4-Phase Participant Accounting
1. **Enrollment:** Assessed for eligibility (`N`) → Excluded (`n`, itemized: not meeting inclusion criteria, declined to participate, other reasons) → Randomized (`N_rand`).
2. **Allocation:** Allocated to Intervention Arm(s) and Control Arm (`n_k`, received allocated intervention vs. did not receive with reasons).
3. **Follow-Up:** Lost to follow-up (`n`, with reasons) + Discontinued intervention (`n`, with reasons).
4. **Analysis:** Analysed (`n`, specifying `Intention-to-Treat [ITT]`, `Modified ITT`, or `Per-Protocol [PP]`, plus any exclusions with reasons).

## 2. Adverse-Event Diverging Butterfly Chart
- Left wing: Experimental / Active Arm (`%` all-grade light bar + overlaid `%` Grade `>= 3` dark bar).
- Right wing: Control / Standard-of-Care Arm (`%` all-grade + `%` Grade `>= 3`).
- Shared central y-axis listing MedDRA / CTCAE preferred terms ordered by frequency.
