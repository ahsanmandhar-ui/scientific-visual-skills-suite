# Medical Education Teaching Poster Standards

## 1. Poster Sizes for Skills Labs & Teaching Wards
- `skills-lab-24x36` (`24.0 × 36.0 in` portrait, `200 DPI` print raster + vector PDF)
- `iso-a1-portrait` (`23.39 × 33.11 in` / `594 × 841 mm`)
- `iso-a2-portrait` (`16.54 × 23.39 in` / `420 × 594 mm`)

## 2. Four High-Yield Pedagogical Modules
1. **Physiology / Pathophysiology Core:** Numbered functional concepts linking normal physiology to clinical signs.
2. **Branching Diagnostic Algorithm:** Entry presentation → initial triage test → binary/ternary decision branches → targeted management.
3. **Pharmacology / Pathology Matrix:** 4-column high-yield comparison table (`Class/Subtype`, `Mechanism/Lesion`, `Clinical Use/Feature`, `Adverse Effect/Pitfall`).
4. **OSCE / OSPE Examination Checklist:** Sequential hands-on steps (`Preparation → Inspection → Palpation/Auscultation → Synthesis`) + **Critical Safety Red Flags**.
