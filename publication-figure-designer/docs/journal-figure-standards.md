## 2. Export & Statistical Presentation Checklist

- **Vector + High-DPI Raster:** Provide `.pdf` and `.svg` for vector line art; provide `600 DPI` `.png`/`.tiff` for combination/line art (`300 DPI` minimum for pure halftone microscopy).
- **Error Bars:** Always state in `figure_legend.md` whether error bars denote `95% CI`, `SD`, `SEM`, or `IQR`, along with exact `n` (biological replicates vs. technical replicates) and the statistical test applied.
- **Spines & Gridlines:** Remove top and right bounding box spines; keep subtle horizontal reference gridlines (`0.4 pt`) only when aiding quantitative lookup.
