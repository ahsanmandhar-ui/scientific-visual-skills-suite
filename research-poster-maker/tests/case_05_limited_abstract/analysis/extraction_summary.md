### 13. Important findings
At 2 hours, persistent delta-Lac >= 1.2 mmol/L occurred in 58/164 (35.4%) patients and was associated with higher 72-hour vasopressor dependency: 62.1% vs 21.7% for delta-Lac < 1.2 mmol/L (unadjusted RR 2.86, 95% CI 1.98–4.13, p = 0.0001).
