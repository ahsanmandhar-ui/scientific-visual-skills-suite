# Cell-Free Methylation Fragmentomics for Early Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma Triage: A Synthetic Translational Study
Authors: Victor K. Sato, PhD; Miriam Alverez, MD
Affiliations: Synthetic Translational Oncology Lab
Results: Locked assay validated on N = 310 plasma samples (110 Stage I-II PDAC, 200 benign pancreatic cysts/controls), sensitivity 87.3% (96/110), specificity 92.5% (185/200).
