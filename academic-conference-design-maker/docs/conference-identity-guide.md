# Academic Conference Visual Identity & Print/Digital Specs

## 1. Standard Asset Dimensions
| Asset | Physical / Pixel Dimensions | Format(s) | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage / Web Banner | `24.0 × 8.0 in` (`3600 × 1200 px`) | PDF, PNG | Podium backdrop, conference portal masthead |
| Abstract-Book Cover | `8.27 × 11.69 in` (`ISO A4`) | Vector PDF, 300-DPI PNG | Printed/PDF Book of Abstracts & Proceedings |
| Event Poster | `18.0 × 24.0 in` (`200 DPI` + Vector PDF) | Vector PDF, PNG | Department noticeboards & digital CFP |
| Certificate | `11.0 × 8.5 in` (Landscape) | Vector PDF, PNG, Editable PPTX | CME / Oral / Poster Presenter credential |
| Speaker Spotlight | `4.0 × 4.0 in` @ 300 DPI (`1200 × 1200 px`) | SVG, PNG | LinkedIn / X / Academic newsletter |
| Social Announcement | `5.333 × 3.0 in` @ 300 DPI (`1600 × 900 px`) | SVG, PNG | Widescreen social & email header |
